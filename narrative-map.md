# Narrative Map — Gina's College of Advanced Aesthetics

**Conversion goal:** Start Your Application / Book a Free Career Consultation
**Audience:** Women 18–35 in Ontario considering a career shift into aesthetics
**Emotional arc:** Curiosity → Recognition → Trust → Desire → Action

---

## Beat 1 — HERO: "Who are you?"
**Visitor's internal question:** Is this the right school, or just another beauty program?
**Emotional shift:** Curious → Impressed in 3 seconds
**Single message:** This is Ontario's most prestigious aesthetics school — and it has been for 45 years.
**Proof type:** Visual (strong editorial photography) + Data (45 years, CIDESCO badge, OSAP eligible)
**Transition to Beat 2:** As hero scrolls out, a bold stat line slides in — graduates working at top med spas and clinics across Canada. This creates the bridge from "who are you" to "why should I care."

---

## Beat 2 — TENSION: "Why should I care?"
**Visitor's internal question:** What does graduating from here actually get me?
**Emotional shift:** Impressed → Motivated
**Single message:** Your career starts the day you graduate — and Gina's graduates are hired first.
**Proof type:** Data (career outcomes, salary ranges, job placement) + Social (where graduates work)
**Copy direction:** Specific, income-forward. "Gina's graduates work in top med spas in Toronto, Ottawa, and across Ontario. Many are booked solid within weeks of graduation."
**Transition to Beat 3:** A subtle color shift from dark navy to near-white as the page moves from aspiration into specifics. The emotional temperature cools slightly — from ambitious to credible.

---

## Beat 3 — MECHANISM: "How is this different?"
**Visitor's internal question:** What makes Gina's better than the school down the street?
**Emotional shift:** Motivated → Convinced
**Single message:** Four things no other school in Ontario can say.
**Proof type:** Experiential (in-house spa with real clients), Institutional (CIDESCO, Ontario Career Colleges Act), Financial (OSAP-eligible), Social (small class sizes)
**Key differentiators to surface:**
1. In-house student spa — real clients, not classmates
2. CIDESCO accreditation — the world standard, Ontario's only career college with it
3. OSAP-eligible — government-backed financial support
4. 45 years of training the industry's top professionals
**Transition to Beat 4:** The mechanism section ends with a program grid teaser — "Explore what you'll learn" — which draws the eye down into the programs section.

---

## Beat 4 — PROGRAMS: "What will I learn?"
**Visitor's internal question:** Is there a program that fits what I want to do?
**Emotional shift:** Convinced → Choosing
**Single message:** Whether you want to specialize in medical aesthetics, hairstyling, or laser technology — there's a full diploma for it.
**Proof type:** Visual (program cards with real imagery) + Data (program length, credentials earned)
**Top 4 diploma programs to feature:**
- Medical Aesthetics — "The industry's highest-earning role. Laser, peels, microneedling."
- Advanced Aesthetics — "The full foundation. Everything the industry expects."
- Hairstyling — "Creative, client-facing, and in constant demand."
- Electrolysis & Light Technology — "Specialized, well-paying, and growing fast."
**Transition to Beat 5:** Social proof follows naturally — "Don't take our word for it."

---

## Beat 5 — PROOF: "Who else trusts you?"
**Visitor's internal question:** Is this real? Did it actually work for someone like me?
**Emotional shift:** Choosing → Confident
**Single message:** Students who weren't sure they could do it — graduated and built careers they love.
**Proof type:** Social (real testimonials, edited for impact, with name + credential)
**Top testimonials to use (edited):**
- Liz M. (Laser Specialist, Ottawa): "I am now employed as a laser specialist in one of the top medical spas in Ottawa. I would highly recommend Gina's College."
- Jessica T.: "My experience could not have been better. I graduated at the top of my class — something I thought I would never achieve."
- Dawn N. (Certified Nail Technician): "Our instructors were excellent and went well above to make our experience enjoyable."
**Transition to Beat 6:** After proof, the temperature rises again. A bold, dark CTA section with a single clear direction.

---

## Beat 6 — ACTION: "What do I do now?"
**Visitor's internal question:** How do I start?
**Emotional shift:** Confident → Committed
**Single message:** Your career starts with one step. Take it today.
**Proof type:** Friction reduction (free consultation, no pressure, campus tours available)
**CTA hierarchy:**
- Primary: "Start Your Application"
- Secondary: "Book a Free Career Consultation"
- Micro: "Talk to an admissions advisor →"
**Design note:** Dark navy background, maximum white space, the two CTAs side by side. No competing content. Just the choice.

---

## Section Transitions

| From → To | Transition technique |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Hero → Tension | Parallax: hero image recedes at 0.85x, stat line slides up at 1.0x |
| Tension → Mechanism | Background crossfade from #01426A to #FFFFFF over 200px scroll |
| Mechanism → Programs | Clip-path wipe: programs section reveals upward as differentiators exit |
| Programs → Proof | Scale recede: program cards scale to 0.97 and dim as testimonials enter |
| Proof → Action | Color snap to deep navy — signals the end of information, start of decision |
